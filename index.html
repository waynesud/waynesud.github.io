Hi, I'm Wayne!

I am a third-year Engineering Physics student at UBC with a focus on electrical design, complemented by experience in software development and mechanical design. My interests include power electronics, sensing, RF design, and automation. I love learning and building stuff!

I am the Electrical Team Lead for UBC Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (UAS), where I design the electrical power and control systems for a heavy-duty autonomous quadcopter. I help research and do design reviews for our RF receiver, video transmitter, main payload, and power distribution PCBs, all of which are produced in-house. I am also responsible for designing a gimbal control board used for camera stabilization and water-turret aiming for this year’s national UAS competition.
